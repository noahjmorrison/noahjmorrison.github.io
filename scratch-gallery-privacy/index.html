Masterpiece Scratch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Masterpiece Scratch is an artwork scratch-and-reveal game for adults, published by Noah Morrison under the SimpleAppsFromTheNorth developer name.

Local app information

The app saves your revealed collection, scratch progress and settings in private storage on your device so you can return to them. It has no account, sign-in, chat or app-operated server for this progress, and no analytics of its own. The app excludes this data from its Android cloud-backup and device-transfer configurations. “Scratch again” resets the current picture’s scratch surface but keeps your collection and settings. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes the app’s local files and preferences. The developer cannot remotely access or remove those copies. Other device-management tools may behave differently.

Advertising and privacy choices

Masterpiece Scratch uses Google AdMob for a small banner on finished-picture screens and occasional full-screen ads between pictures. Ads do not interrupt scratching or art tours. A one-time Google Play purchase removes both formats. Google's advertising software may process your IP address, which can indicate approximate location, the Android advertising ID where available, app set ID, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics or performance information. Advertising information is used for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Google documents app set ID for analytics and fraud prevention; the app does not read that identifier itself or link it to your gallery progress. Processing depends on device settings, applicable privacy choices and Google's services.

When required, the app asks for privacy choices before requesting an ad and offers a way to revisit the choices. Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily remove all advertising. The gallery remains usable when privacy checks, internet access or advertising fail. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising practices. Android also provides advertising ID controls.

Other features and links

The app does not request your photos, microphone or precise location, and offers an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. It does not send your collection or artwork choices to an app-operated server. Artwork and tours are included for offline use. Opening a museum source link leaves the app; that website's practices then apply.

Optional purchase to remove ads

Google Play processes the optional one-time purchase. The app uses purchase information returned by Google Play to grant or restore ad removal and saves a local ad-removal status on your device. The developer does not receive your payment card details or operate a purchase server. Google Play maintains its own purchase and payment records under Google's policies. Restoring a purchase requires access to the Google Play account used for the purchase. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local status but does not cancel a completed purchase or remove Google-held purchase records. The app can restore eligible purchases through Google Play.

Contact and support email

Contact Noah Morrison at simpleappsfromthenorth@gmail.com with support or privacy questions. If you email the developer, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send and uses it to handle your enquiry. Please do not send sensitive information that is not needed for that purpose.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not delete emails sent to support or records held by Google.

The developer does not control Google’s advertising records. For Google-held information, see Google privacy choices and Google privacy requests.

International processing and changes

Google operates internationally and may process advertising information outside your country. The developer does not have an app-specific list of countries used for each advertising request. Google describes its safeguards, retention and international processing in its privacy policy and data transfer explanation. Applicable privacy rights still apply.

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be reassessed and updated. Contact Noah Morrison at simpleappsfromthenorth@gmail.com with privacy questions.
